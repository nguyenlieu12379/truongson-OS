# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS (plus minimal vanilla JS), no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages. Confirmed by Son, 2026-11-06.

## Users
US small business owners (any trade: cafés, restaurants, barbers, contractors, shops). They usually arrive straight from a TruongSon cold call or cold email, on their phone, between tasks. They are skeptical of agencies and short on time. Their job on the page: decide in about 10 seconds whether TruongSon is legit and worth a 15-minute call.

## Product Purpose
TruongSon builds websites for small businesses. The agency site backs up cold outreach. Success = the visitor books a 15-minute Google Meet through the Google Calendar booking page.

## Positioning
Zero risk. TruongSon builds the first draft of your site before you pay anything. If you don't love the design, you walk away and owe nothing. ("Love the design or you don't pay.")

## Operating Context
- Outreach: cold calls and Gmail, then sales calls on Google Meet.
- Booking: Google Calendar appointment schedule, 15-minute slots with auto Meet link. Real URL pending (placeholder in build).
- Payments later via Stripe (not set up).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- No prices on the site. Quote happens on the call.
- Copy is general small business, not F&B-specific.
- One CTA: book the call. No phone or email shown.
- Pricing undecided.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: TruongSon. Co-founders Son and Truong.
- Voice: casual but professional, short sentences, no em dashes, no fluff (see `references/voice.md`).

## Evidence on Hand
- None yet. Pre-revenue: no clients, testimonials, case studies, logos, or portfolio. Do not fabricate any of these.
- The guarantee is the proof.

## Product Principles
1. Remove risk before asking for anything.
2. Fast trust for a skeptical, busy owner on a phone.
3. Talk like a person, not an agency.
4. One action: book the call.
