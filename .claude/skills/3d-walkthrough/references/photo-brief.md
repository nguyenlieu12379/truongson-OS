# Photo brief (send to the owner)

What paying clients send us, taken on their own phone in about 10 minutes. These photos are the design of their walkthrough: every surface in the final clips comes from them. Their own photos are also the only ones we can publish.

Send the owner the "For the owner" part below as is (a message, email or doc). Fill in the bracketed bits.

## For the owner

Hi [name], to build your walkthrough we need photos of [venue]. Your phone is perfect. It takes about 10 minutes.

**When:** a normal day, lights on, ideally morning or early afternoon. No customers in the shot if you can.

**How to shoot every photo:**
- Hold the phone sideways (landscape).
- Stand and hold it at eye height. Don't tilt up or down.
- Keep it steady and in focus. No zoom, no filters, no portrait mode.
- Avoid glare: don't shoot straight into a window or a mirror.
- Two of each spot is fine. More is better than fewer.

**The shots, in walking order:**

| # | Spot | What we need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Street | From across the street: the whole front of the building and the sidewalk |
| 2 | Front | Closer: storefront, sign, door, any outdoor seats |
| 3 | Door | Standing at the door, looking in |
| 4 | Room | From just inside the door, the whole room. Then one from each corner |
| 5 | Bar or counter | Straight on, then from each side |
| 6 | Hero spot | Your signature spot (espresso machine, oven, bar, pastry case), close and wide |
| 7 | Seating | Tables, stools, booths, the patio if you have one |
| 8 | Details | Floor, walls, lights, menu board, plants: one close-up each |
| 9 | Walk video | Walk slowly from the sidewalk, through the door, to the counter. 20 to 30 seconds, phone sideways, no talking needed |

**Send them** as original files (not screenshots), in a shared folder or by email. Tell us anything you'd like left out.

Thanks!

## For us (when they arrive)

- Check every chapter of the route has at least 2 sharp photos: street, front, door, room, bar or counter, hero spot.
- Drop photos with text on glass, strong reflections, people in front, or blur. Ask for a reshoot of any chapter left with fewer than 2.
- Use the walk video to check the layout and route (step 2), not as a design photo.
- Save them in `projects/<venue>/photos/` (client-owned, fine to publish on their site).
