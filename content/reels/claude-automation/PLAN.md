# PLAN: "Automate properly with Claude Code" reel

## Idea in one line

A retro desk computer walks you through 5 rules for real automation with Claude Code, one rule per beat, and ends by typing COMMENT AUTOMATE.

## Format

- 1080x1920, 30 fps, 45 s (1350 frames). Instagram reel, posted directly.
- Voice: Kokoro TTS (free, local), natural speed.
- Captions: word by word from the voice timings, burned in, lower third, cream box with dark text.
- Sound: one effect per visible action (made in code, free), soft music bed made in code, ducked under the voice. Master at -14 LUFS.

## Script (about 100 words)

> Stop using Claude Code like a chatbot. Here's how to automate properly.
> One. Pick a task you repeat every week. Not your whole job. One task.
> Two. Write the steps down once, as a skill. Claude follows it the same way every time.
> Three. Give it real tools. Connect your apps with MCP, or let it run a script.
> Four. Make it check its own work. Tests, screenshots, a second look.
> Five. Put it on a trigger. A schedule or a hook, so it runs without you.
> When it gets something wrong, fix the skill. Not the output.
> Comment AUTOMATE and we'll send you the checklist.

## Contrast (the hook)

0-4 s: the screen shows a chat bubble, "can you help me with...", and "chatbot" gets a red strike-through. Then the screen clears to a clean terminal. Bad habit, then the fix.

## Hook

Frame 1: the CRT pops in (grows in 0.25 s, squashes, settles), the keyboard lands 0.2 s later, the lamp flickers on at 0.8 s.
