# Tōv Coffee: brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Son: "as each F&B clients make one for another, use the scroll craft to build with its own vibe. Don't copy from each website for another because it may not fit." Earlier standing direction for these demos: pick the prospect, find inputs online, build, full auto. No interview was run; every answer below is my decision, grounded in Tōv's own material (their printed menu, their space, their history).

Not copied from Espresso Vivace: no shared markup, CSS, layout, palette, fonts or interactions.

## The business (evidence)

- Egyptian coffee shop, 3639 SE Hawthorne Blvd, Portland. Opened 2015 in a red double-decker bus by Joseph Nazir, moved into the shop in late 2023 (Wikipedia).
- No website. Linktree to Instagram @tovcoffee_co. Yelp hours changed several times in 2026.
- Their printed menu (Commons photo, June 2025): house drinks with names like Nectar of the Gods and Chocolate Jesus, cardamom-infused espresso, and a note: "Due to the complexity of each drink, things could pile up and your simple drip or latte may take up to 20 minutes."
- Their room (Commons photos, 2025): violet walls with gold moulding, red kilim floor cushions, a brass tray, lanterns, a palm.

## The eight topics (authored)

1. **Vibe:** a violet room you walk into. Ornate, warm, unhurried, a bit funny. References: the room itself, a Cairo coffeehouse doorway, a London bus destination blind.
2. **Journey:** step through the door into the lounge. Their own warning that your drink takes a while. The drinks, by name. The rest of the house. Where it started (the bus). Last stop: the address, open or not, how to get there or order.
3. **Energy:** calm open, one big movement (the doorway), then easy and slow. Nothing loud after the hero.
4. **Feeling:** see curve. Remember: walking through the arch into the purple room.
5. **What no other site does:** the navigation is a bus destination blind that rolls to wherever you are, because Tōv started as a bus. The last stop is their door.
6. **Range:** maximalist, warm. Pattern, gold rules, deep violet, big named drinks. Not premium-minimal.
7. **World:** distinct stops, not one continuous world. A bus route: each section is a stop with its own ground, hard arrivals.
8. **Assets:** Wikimedia Commons photos by Another Believer (CC BY-SA 4.0): lounge, hall, window seat, counter, storefront, the bus. No video, no generation, no spend.

## Grammar: Route line (new)

The page is a bus route. Defined constraints:

- **Navigation:** a fixed destination blind (the only chrome besides the wordmark). It rolls to the current stop's name as you scroll, and opens into the full list of stops, each a jump link.
- **Sequence:** stops in a fixed order, each on its own hard ground (no drift). Arrivals are cuts, not crossfades.
- **Ending:** the terminus. The blind reads the street address and whether the doors are open now; the screen holds address, hours, map, directions, phone and ordering.
- **Bans:** `scrub` (no footage), pinned crossfade type acts, `spotlight`, `magnet`, `kinetic`, `count`, `drift`, chapter numbers.
- **Leans on:** one bespoke pinned hero, `pan` for the menu, `reveal` for the house, `flow` + `in`, `parallax`.

Why the other grammars lost: filmic one-shot and continuous world need footage or a world we don't have; live surface is for software; typographic poster wastes the room photos; split stage has no two sides; rhythmic cutlist fights the brand's "made slow"; chaptered editorial was Vivace v1's grammar; gallery/catalog was close (the menu is a collection) but drinks have no photos and the visit is the point, not the range.

## Feeling curve

1. **Curiosity:** a violet wall with a pointed arch; through it, a glimpse of red cushions and a brass tray.
2. **Transport (peak):** the wall and its jamb grow past you at different rates and you are standing in the lounge.
3. **Amusement:** their own warning, set big: your latte may take up to 20 minutes. Quiet gold ground. Authored silence before the menu.
4. **Appetite:** the drinks slide past on gold-framed plates, each with its real name and what's in it.
5. **Warmth:** the rest of the house, revealed in wipes: the hall, the window seat, the counter.
6. **Nostalgia:** the red double-decker where it began.
7. **Arrival:** the blind rolls to the address. Open now or when it opens, the map, the door.

**Peak:** "you scroll and walk through the arch into this purple room full of cushions." Act 1, the largest span on the page (3.2vh).

**It's the site where** the sign at the top rolls like a bus blind as you go, and you get off at their front door.

**Authored silence:** act 2 (the 20-minute note) is deliberately sparse.
