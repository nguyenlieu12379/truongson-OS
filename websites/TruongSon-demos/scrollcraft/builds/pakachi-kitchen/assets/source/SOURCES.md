## Cutouts and logo

- `cut-tray.webp`: the foil tray in pk-biryani-tray.jpg, traced along its rim (hand-traced polygon, edges refined with GrabCut, no AI fill). Its right side from the middle down and its bottom edge are the photo's own edge, so the page keeps those edges off screen or inside a card frame.
- `cut-lemon-a.webp`, `cut-lemon-b.webp`: two lemon slices from pk-haleem.jpg, ellipse-traced and refined.
- `logo-gold.webp`: their logo (Facebook photo 1151497867450901, a gold-on-white render), with the white removed by colour. "SOUL OF KARACHI" under the name was too thin to keep, so the page sets it in type.
- `logo-ring.webp`: the ring and wheat sprig only, from the same file.
- `icon-64.png`, `icon-180.png`, `emblem.png`: the cloche from their logo on black.

Not used on purpose: weekly menu flyers, review screenshots, posts with names or people, reels (720 to 1080px).
