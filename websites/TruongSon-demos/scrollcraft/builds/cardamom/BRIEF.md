## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** match their IG as a serious restaurant. Authored words: warm, late, generous, Hyderabadi. References: a green neon sign on a strip-mall street after midnight; a biryani plate at the end of a long night.
2. **Journey (authored):** the plate you came for > what else to order, with prices > they're open when everyone else is closed > the room, for families, catering and banquets > where, when, order.
3. **Energy:** calm hero, steady menu, the big lift at the sign, warm and quiet inside, calm close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see curve below.
5. **Signature move (authored):** the sign switches on. The menu's last row is Irani chai; its photo is their storefront with IRANI CHAI in the window and the neon sign off. The frame grows, the page goes to night and their real green sign comes on letter by letter, flickering.
6. **Style:** serious. Fraunces (text serif, no novelty) and Plus Jakarta Sans. Their green at full strength in type, gold on the button only.
7. **World:** one unbroken world on a fixed stage. Nothing scrolls up; scroll drives motion.
8. **Assets:** their own reel frames and one poster dish (cut along the plate, no poster text). See `assets/source/SOURCES.md`.

## Grammar
**The sign (light as the axis).** One fixed stage that goes from day to night. The night is the content (they're open late), and the switch is their own sign in their own photo. Why not the others: the table (cantinho) needs one great overhead photo they don't have; the pass and the comal are taken; the flag fits El Salvador; filmic one-shot needs clean footage (their reels carry logo overlays and captions).

## Failure checks (FAILURES.md)
- Picture + letter on a side all page: the menu act is frame + list, but it's one of five forms; hero is centred and layered, the peak is a light change, the close is a reprise.
- Washed beige, blur fill: no blur. Neutral off-white ground, then real night.
- Floating cards without reason: one card, at the close, beside the dish it sells.
- Same label+sentence on every beat: menu rows, an hours table, a services list and a card are four different forms.
- Novelty fonts / toy mechanic: Fraunces and Plus Jakarta Sans. The mechanic is their own sign in their own photo and carries a fact (hours).
- Cutout whole? The plate is the only cutout; it is fully inside its poster, nothing in front, checked on white, black and off-white (`lab/check-biryani.jpg`). Other photos are framed, not cut.
- Value: every act answers something: what to order and for how much, when they're open, what the room is for, where, how to order.
- Toggle in the far corner; CTA before it.
- Gap: no drifting cutouts. No second dish in their posts is whole and clean enough to cut.
- Gap: the hero plate is a poster image (studio look, likely retouched or generated by their designer). It is their own post; flag to Son.
