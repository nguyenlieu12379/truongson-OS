## Grammar
**The table (camera distance).** One fixed stage; the subject is one real photo seen from different distances, then the camera carries sideways along the hot line. Why not the others: the pass (tardes) and the comal (el-salvadoreno) are taken; counter catalog needs a tag-bearing case; flag bands fit El Salvador, not this; filmic one-shot needs footage we don't have clean. The pull-back is the honest move for one great overhead photo.
