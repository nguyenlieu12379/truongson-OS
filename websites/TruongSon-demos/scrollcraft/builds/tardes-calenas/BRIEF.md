## Grammar
**The pass.** One fixed stage where plates are served across it like a kitchen pass: each plate slides in, stops in the centre with its name, what's on it and its price, then carries on out as the next one comes in. Plates are never shown in frames; they sit on the ground as real cut plates with a soft contact shadow. Why not the others: counter catalog is Gochón; orbit is Sweets; clock is El Budare (failed); filmic one-shot needs footage they don't have; chaptered editorial breaks "unbroken"; typographic poster would bury the food; rhythmic cutlist fights "super smooth".

## Signature move
**The table builds itself.** At the peak, the plates that passed come back and set themselves around the Picada, from above, like a table for six at 2AM. Each lands with its name and price beside it. It's the menu turned into one table you'd actually order.

## Failure checks (FAILURES.md)
- **Picture + text column for the whole page:** no. The plate moves centre, left, through, into a ring, out to the edges; copy anchors change every scene.
- **Washed beige, brand as thin accent:** ground is neutral off-white (standing rule), but yellow is used at full strength on huge type and fills, red on the CTA.
- **Layers with no relationship:** every overlap means something: plate in front of the letters, labels tied to plate parts by leader lines, plates arranged as a table.
- **Novelty mechanic as centrepiece (El Budare clock):** the centrepiece is their food and their week. No clock, no tickets.
- **Playful fonts:** confident text faces only (Archivo, Inter). No script, no cartoon.
- **Toggle mid-bar:** toggle in the far corner, CTA docked beside it.
- **Empty of value:** every scene gives a name, a price, an hour, a night or an address.
- **Incomplete cutouts:** every cut checked alone on white and black, whole plate in frame, nothing in front.
- **Cutouts floating on blank:** plates sit on the ground with a real contact shadow, arranged as a table; a full plate on a light ground reads as "served", not as a sticker.
- **Same hero formula:** the word is split around the plate (above and below), not a single giant word behind it.
- **Would a 3,000-review restaurant owner show this?** That's the bar: calm type, full-size food, one action.
