## Grammar and gate

- Grammar: **Orbit stage**. One fixed stage around a vertical axis; layers ride an ellipse in 3D. No lateral pans, no fans, no z flythrough, no sections in flow.
- Nav: the avatar's round logo rolls along a thin rail at the bottom to the current stop (5 clickable stops). Bar: "Sweets" lands in it at beat 2, theme toggle, travelling button.
- Hero: product cutout in front of a giant wordmark with covered letters animating. **Same device as Delícias and Pakachi, by request (PREFS 5).**
- Act sequence: hero > wordmark docks + cake to centre > orbit of 8 > fast spin with names (peak) > macro iris > four treats served > reprise close with map.
- Close: the hero reassembles (wordmark undocks back behind the cake) with the order card and a live Google map. Holds.
- Signature: the cake turntable.
- Gate: vs Pakachi differs on grammar, nav, act sequence, close, signature (5 of 6). vs Delícias: grammar, nav, act sequence, close, signature (5 of 6). vs Brazilian Passion Foods v2: all 6. vs Vivace: all 6.

## How this build avoids every pattern in FAILURES.md

| Failure | This build |
|---|---|
| One photo panel plus a text column for the whole scroll | No layout repeats: wordmark + cutout, a 3D orbit, a full-bleed macro, a served row of four, a reprise with a map. |
| Blurred-photo or gradient fill backgrounds | Flat blush (or script black). No blur behind anything. The only soft shape is the turntable's shadow ellipse under the orbit, which grounds the cakes. |
| Floating cards and pills with no reason | The cards are cakes on a turntable: they share one ellipse, pass in front of and behind the centre cake by depth, and cast onto the same shadow. |
| Muted beige palette with a thin brand accent | Their script black carries the type and the wordmark at full strength; their watercolour rose and the hibiscus rose come from the avatar and the cake itself. |
| Same small label + big sentence every beat | Copy changes shape: brush wordmark, theme names on the orbit, a giant flipping script line, a two-line macro caption, dish names under frames, an order card. |
| Plastic drawn props | None. Cake, flowers, photos are theirs. The only drawn things are hairlines, the shadow ellipse and type. |
| "Would this fit any other business?" | No: their flamingo cake, their themed custom cakes spinning on a turntable, their alfajores. |

Food is never under letters: the cake is in front of "Sweets"; captions sit under frames.
