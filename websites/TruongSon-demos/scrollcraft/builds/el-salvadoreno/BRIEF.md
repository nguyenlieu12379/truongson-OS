## Failure checks (FAILURES.md)
- **Picture + text column for the whole page:** no. The pupusa moves right, left, multiplies, steps aside; the copy anchor changes every scene; the plates are a spread, the builder is a working card.
- **Washed beige, brand as thin accent:** navy at full strength on huge type and fills, gold on the CTA; ground is a neutral off-white per standing rule.
- **Layers with no relationship:** the pupusa sits in front of the brand word on purpose; the copies land on the real tray; one plate breaks the spread edge to lead into the builder.
- **Novelty mechanic as centrepiece (El Budare):** the centrepiece is their pupusa and their prices. The builder is a real ordering aid, not a toy.
- **Cartoon fonts / sticker cutouts:** DM Serif Display + Manrope. The pupusa sits with a soft contact shadow, never floating on blank white.
- **Cutout never whole:** the pupusa is fully in frame in the source; checked on white and black before use.
