## Grammar
**The flag.** One fixed stage made of three horizontal bands, blue, white, blue, like the Salvadoran flag on their avatar. The bands never leave; they change proportion to frame each scene. Blue bands carry type (the name, categories, prices). The white band carries the food. Bans: no cards floating on blank space, no section that scrolls up, no fades between scenes.
Why not the others: the pass is Tardes; counter catalog is Gochón; orbit is Sweets; clock is El Budare (failed); continuous z-world is Delícias; stage choreography with a dealt hand is Pakachi; filmic one-shot needs footage; chaptered editorial breaks "unbroken".

## Signature move
**The flag carries the menu.** At the peak the white band opens to half the screen and the whole kitchen travels across it under your scroll: pupusa, pupusa ranchera, chicharrones, chuleta, camarones, pargo, jamaica, each with its name and real price. The blue band above runs the categories one way, the band below runs the hours and the order line the other way. The flag becomes a moving menu board.

## Failure checks (FAILURES.md)
- **Picture + text column for the whole page (Brazilian Passion):** no. The composition changes every scene: emblem centred, close-up left, a moving board, a full-bleed tray, emblem and card.
- **Washed beige, brand as thin accent:** flag blue at full strength over a third to half of every screen.
- **Layers with no relationship:** the pupusa sits on the flag as its emblem and crosses the band edges on purpose; the card straddles the top band's edge.
- **Novelty mechanic as centrepiece, not serious (El Budare):** no clock, tickets or chips. The centrepiece is their food and their flag. Condensed signage type and a serif, no cartoon fonts.
- **Empty of value:** every scene answers something: what it is, what's in it, what it costs, party trays, hours, where, how to order.
- **Toggle stranded mid-bar:** theme toggle is last in the bar, far corner.
- **Cutout never whole:** only plates fully inside their photo were cut (fitted outline, checked on off-white and blue: `lab/cuts-sheet.png`). Plates that touched the photo edge were dropped.
